Who we are

Fluid: Dual N-Back is developed by Monitor Lizards, operated by Valentyn Khast. This policy covers the Fluid app and the Fluid pages at monitorlizards.studio/fluid. For privacy questions or requests, contact nest@monitorlizards.studio.

What stays on your device

Fluid saves completed rounds, scores, practice dates, n levels, history, lesson progress and your preferences on your device. Detailed local round records can also contain the exercise sequence, responses and timing information used to calculate results and diagnose interrupted rounds. These records let Fluid show your progress and restore your choices.

Sounds, letter recordings and music are bundled with the app. Fluid plays audio; it does not record your microphone. It does not request access to your contacts, photos or location. We do not add advertising, tracking, developer analytics or a developer crash-upload service to the app.

Apple App Store editions

Fluid for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV saves practice on the device. Versions with Sync with iCloud can also store completed Classic results in the private CloudKit database of the Apple Account signed in on the device. Use the same Apple Account and a compatible version of Fluid on each device to sync. Fluid does not need a separate login. Apple versions without the sync setting keep practice locally. Apple device backups are managed separately in system settings.

Synced results include the round identifier, n levels, scores, practice date, completion timestamps and time-zone offset, sequence seed, pause count and voice-pack identifier. CloudKit stores the round payload in an encrypted field. Your detailed responses, diagnostic timing, lesson progress and audio preferences stay on the device. Monitor Lizards does not receive the synced rounds or have access to this private database. Apple TV keeps device-only rounds separate, and tvOS may clear cached copies of restored history to make room.

Apple handles App Store purchases, TestFlight distribution and platform diagnostics under its own terms and your settings. We do not receive your payment card information. TestFlight feedback and diagnostics you share through Apple may be made available to us through Apple's services.

Steam Cloud and shared practice

Steam editions can use Valve's Steamworks services and your Steam account to keep progress available on your Mac and Windows computers. When Steam Cloud is enabled and available, Fluid uploads completed-round records and selected preferences to your private Steam Cloud storage. Records include scores, n level, round identifier and sequence seed, timing and date information, pause count and voice identifier. Full local stimulus, response and diagnostic timing evidence is excluded from the Cloud record. Cloud data is associated with your Steam account and a randomly generated app-writer identifier.

Where Steam Friends sharing is enabled, Fluid can publish a compact practice summary to Steam leaderboards: recent daily round counts, n totals and peaks, rolling average n, lifetime round totals and peak or best-cleared Classic n, streaks, activity dates and data-coverage information. Where a first-share notice is shown, sharing begins after you accept it; later eligible updates can publish automatically from the foreground menus. The Friends view reads Steam identifiers and display names to show summaries for your current Steam friends. Your full round-by-round history is not included in these shared summaries. Private Cloud records used to validate shared summaries also contain round identifiers, record hashes and account/writer identifiers.

Leaderboard summaries are associated with your Steam profile. Do not treat them as confidential: Steam controls access to its leaderboards. Fluid does not currently provide an in-app hide or remove-shared-stats control. Disabling Steam Cloud stops Cloud synchronization; it does not erase previously published leaderboard entries. Steam Cloud settings and Steam's own privacy controls are managed through Steam.

In Steam builds with achievements enabled, Fluid checks eligibility from saved practice and sends earned achievement unlocks to Steam. Steam associates unlocks with the signed-in account and controls their profile visibility. This sends the achievement earned; Steam Cloud and optional Friends sharing handle practice data separately.

Monitor Lizards does not operate a separate account or progress server for Fluid. Valve operates Steam's account, Cloud and leaderboard services under its own policy. Store purchases are handled by Steam; we do not receive your payment card information.

When you contact us

If you email us or send feedback, we receive the address and information you choose to include. We use it to answer your request, troubleshoot the app and maintain necessary support records. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information or complete practice files unless needed for your request.

We keep support correspondence only as long as reasonably needed to resolve and follow up on the issue, meet applicable legal requirements or handle a dispute. You can request access, correction or deletion by contacting nest@monitorlizards.studio. We may ask for enough information to verify a request, without asking for your store password.

This website

The Fluid pages do not use advertising, analytics, tracking pixels or embedded social/video players. Fonts and artwork are served with the site. The motion control stores only your animation preference in this browser when browser storage is available.

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub receives ordinary connection information and logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. Links to stores, research papers and other sites take you to services governed by their own privacy policies. No store, research or video service is embedded or contacted automatically by the Fluid pages.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Local practice and preferences remain on your device until removed. Deleting the iPhone or iPad app normally removes its local app data; offloading an app can retain data. System backups may retain earlier copies. On desktop, uninstalling the game may leave its local data directory and Steam Cloud data in place. On Apple versions with iCloud sync, open Settings → Manage practice data to turn sync off or erase data. Turning sync off stops transfers from that device. Erase device and iCloud data removes the current account’s Fluid history from iCloud after Apple confirms deletion. Synced copies on other devices may remain until those devices reconnect with sync on. On iPhone, iPad and Mac, you can also export your history as JSON. To export synced rounds from Apple TV, use one of those devices with the same Apple Account. Contact us if you need help identifying and removing your Fluid data.

Steam Cloud files and leaderboard entries can remain with Valve after the local app is removed. For requests about data held by Apple, Valve or GitHub, their account tools and privacy contacts may also be needed. We cannot promise to erase records controlled by those services. We do not sell your personal data or use it for targeted advertising.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, erase or receive a copy of personal data, object to processing or complain to your local data-protection authority. Contact us to exercise rights relating to data we handle. Where applicable, we process voluntary support information to provide requested assistance, maintain and protect the service, and meet legal obligations. Service providers may process data in other countries under their own safeguards and policies.

Children and policy changes

Fluid is not designed to collect personal information from children. Do not send us a child's personal information unnecessarily; contact us if you believe it has been provided so we can address it.

If we change how Fluid handles data, we will update this policy and its date, and any required store disclosures. You can read the policy bundled with your version of Fluid offline in Settings → Privacy policy. The latest policy is available at this address.

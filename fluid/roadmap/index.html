Fluid starts with one thing done well: a beautiful place to practise dual n-back. Here is what is in the app, and where we plan to take it.

Updated 17 November 2026

Already in Fluid

Adaptive Classic rounds, optional replayable lessons, position and sound scores, daily progress charts, and a quiet interface with adjustable audio and motion.

The first iPhone and iPad release saves rounds, settings and learning progress on the device. You can practise offline without creating a Fluid account. Removing the app can remove its local data; Apple system backups are separate from a Fluid sync feature.

Planned: Apple-device progress sync

We plan to let you continue your iPhone and iPad practice across your Apple devices through iCloud, without an extra Fluid login. This is a future update, not a feature of the first iOS release.

Keeping existing progress safe, working offline and handling unavailable iCloud storage are part of that work. We will share availability here once the feature is ready and tested. No release date is announced.

Steam progress stays in the Steam ecosystem. This plan does not connect Steam and mobile accounts.

On the horizon

More recorded voices and interface languages. A Sandbox for exploring different memory depths, speeds and additional stimulus streams. More ways to personalise the visual atmosphere.

These are ideas in our backlog, not announced features or a fixed delivery order. The core practice comes first.

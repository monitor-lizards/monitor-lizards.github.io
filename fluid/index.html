01 / A simple rule. A real challenge.

Two signals.
One focus.

Watch where the circle appears. Listen to the letter. Respond when either repeats from n steps earlier. Sometimes both match. Sometimes neither does.

01 / Position

Keep the place.

Follow positions around the centre. A familiar place is a match only when it appeared exactly n steps ago.

02 / Sound

Keep the sound.

Listen to a sequence of spoken letters. Track it independently from what you see.

03 / Depth
n = 2

Find your level.

At 2-back, compare with two steps ago. Classic adjusts the depth after each completed round.

03 / The research

Curiosity.
With substance.

Dual n-back is a working-memory exercise studied in research on fluid intelligence: the ability to reason through unfamiliar problems.

Explore the research ↗

Early studies reported improvements on fluid-intelligence tests after adaptive training. Later studies have found a more varied picture. Fluid brings the exercise into a focused, repeatable practice.

Jaeggi et al. / PNAS / 2008Improving fluid intelligence with training on working memory ↗The foundational dual n-back training study. Soveri et al. / 2017Working memory training revisited ↗A meta-analysis of n-back training and transfer.

Fluid's scores measure performance on the exercise. Fluid itself has not been clinically tested; practice does not guarantee wider intelligence gains.

A few things to know

Do I need to know dual n-back already?

No. Start with a single position or sound stream in Learn. All lessons are optional and replayable, and their short practice does not change your Classic progress.

Is this an intelligence test?

No. Your n level and scores describe your performance in this exercise. They are not an IQ score or a clinical assessment. Read the research notes for the wider context.

Can I practise offline?

Yes. The exercise, lessons and letter recordings work locally. Steam Cloud and Friends need Steam connectivity. The current iOS version saves progress on the device.

What happens to my data?

Fluid has no advertising or separate app login. Local storage and Steam sharing work differently; the privacy policy explains both in plain language.

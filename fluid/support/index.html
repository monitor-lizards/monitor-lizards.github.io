Learning and Classic

Open Learn for optional, replayable lessons. Its short practice does not count toward Classic progress. Classic adapts your level using separate position and sound scores. The playing guide explains matches, depth and the score thresholds.

Sound and interruptions

Preview the letter recordings in Settings. Use a comfortable device volume, then adjust music and interface sounds independently. Music can be disabled during practice. Optional focus and output-change guards can restart a round when you switch apps or audio devices; their descriptions in Settings explain the behavior.

A real audio interruption, playback failure, sleep or backgrounding on a phone can stop a round to avoid saving a misleading result.

Where is my progress?

Progress shows completed Classic rounds and daily charts. The current iPhone and iPad version saves on the device. Apple-device sync is planned, not available yet. Device backups are controlled by Apple and are separate from an in-app sync feature.

Steam editions use your Steam account and Steam Cloud when enabled and available. Start Steam online and allow synchronization to finish before changing computers. A store account on another platform does not automatically link to Steam. Read the storage and sharing details.

Privacy or deletion requests

Use the support email for questions about personal data or help removing local records. Removing an app does not necessarily remove platform backups, Steam Cloud files or shared leaderboard entries. Fluid currently has no in-app delete-all-data control. The privacy policy explains the available choices and provider responsibilities.

Platforms and availability

Steam for Mac and Windows and the iPhone/iPad release are in preparation. The availability section will link to each public listing when it is live. Android and additional PC stores are possible later destinations; they are not currently offered for download.

Friends testing invitations are managed separately. No private invitation is published on this site.
